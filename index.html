Scope. Verdicts cover completion timing against the declared secrets — not power, EM, cache, or microarchitectural channels. The analysis is a syntactic fan-in cone including every enclosing if/case guard, so within the supported subset it over-approximates: CONSTANT_TIME is conservative there, and LEAKY names the reaching signals so you can confirm rather than take it on faith.

The supported subset is one flat module of assign statements, net declarations with initialisers, and always blocks. Submodule instantiations, for loops, generate, function and task definitions, and preprocessor macros are not read — each would hide dependency edges, and a hidden edge makes a leaky design look safe. Rather than analyse what it can and stay quiet about the rest, the checker returns UNKNOWN and says which construct stopped it. UNKNOWN is not a pass.
